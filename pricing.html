Questions

Nothing hidden in the fine print.

Is Founding Supporter a subscription?

No. It is a one-time $5 contribution during the founding period.

Will Founding Supporter become Pro automatically?

No. Founding Supporter is a permanent early-supporter status. Pro will be a separate ongoing tier. An account can eventually have both.

Do I need to pay to use PAI?

No. PAI currently has a Free tier during active development.

Why is Pro still TBD?

Because the premium feature set and pricing should be based on actual product value, not a placeholder subscription sold before the benefits are ready.
