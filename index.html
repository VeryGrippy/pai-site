PAI owns the process, not just the prompt.

The goal is simple: give PAI an idea, broken code, or an existing project and let it handle the path to a verified result.

01

Understand & plan

PAI maps the project, identifies the languages and frameworks, and turns your request into concrete requirements before editing.

02

Build & repair

It creates or edits the right files, runs the project, reads real failures, and repairs problems instead of guessing that the code works.

03

Verify & finish

Compiler results, tests, requirements, and cross-file contracts become evidence that the requested work is actually complete.

Install PAI once. Update inside the app.

The public updater is being completed for the PAI 6.9 distribution release.
